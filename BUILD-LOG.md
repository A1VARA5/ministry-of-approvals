- Workflow `ministry-approval` written with defineWorkflow: intake (Front Desk) -> department-a (Pedantry, AI effect) -> department-b (Rubber Stamps, AI effect) -> department-c (Archive, AI effect, loses every third serial once) -> minister-review (human: approve / reject with reason / refer to a department, plus an assignee field for who is Minister) -> certified (issue-certificate effect, then announce effect that POSTs a Discord webhook with retry 3x exponential) -> on-the-wall. Withdraw at the desk ends in shredded. Any failed clerk escalates to the Minister. `sanity-workflows deploy --check` passed first time. Deployed as v1 with the CLI.
- Effect handlers: each clerk loads its clerk document by role and calls `client.agent.action.prompt` (apiVersion vX, format json, the form passed as a groq instructionParam so the model only sees title, kind, body, citizen, serial, amendments). Outputs are typed (complete, lost, remark) and the transitions read them; remarks are also written back to workflow fields with field.set ops so the status page and the certificate can show them. The rubber stamp and the archivist fall back to their catchphrase if the model call fails; the pedantic clerk does not, a failure there escalates to the Minister.
- Local drainer (workflows/src/drain-local.ts): sweeps instances with pending effects at start, then `client.listen` on instances with unclaimed effects and calls drainEffects per instance. This is the laptop stand in for the Sanity Function.
